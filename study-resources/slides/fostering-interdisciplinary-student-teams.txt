Three parts
What happened. Team members often disagree about the facts. Each person thinks that they are correct. Do not try to prove who is correct. Try to learn how the other person sees the situation.
Feelings. Frustration, anxiety, and resentment are usually below the surface. If you ignore them, they come out in other ways, for example in tone or in silence.
Identity. A conflict can make a person ask, "Am I competent? Am I a good teammate?" This is why small disagreements can feel large. Expect this in yourself and in others.

Peer Grading Mechanics

We discard complains without explanation and those beyond team citizenship (e.g, regarding ability or effort)

See form to preview effects
Can lead to substantial grade adjustments (-10% to -50% common)

Depending on severity, TAs will escalate to instructors
